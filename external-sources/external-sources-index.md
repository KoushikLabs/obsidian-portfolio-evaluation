# External Sources

Foundational, published guidance on **portfolio-level Monitoring, Evaluation and
Learning (MEL)**. These are the sources to read *outside* the conference
material — the durable references that a funder/incubator adviser should know.

The two Norad/Itad items are the **Week 1 foundational readings** in the
[[learning-plan|learning plan]].

---

## ⚠️ Source-handling note (read first)

- **Conference slides are private.** The slides in the
  `Conference-2026-slides-for-sharing/` folder (UK Evaluation Society 2026
  sessions) are a **private, personal-reference-only source. They must NOT be
  redistributed, quoted publicly, or shared outside personal study.** The
  [[case-studies/case-studies-index|case studies]] are written from the *public* conference
  programme page, not from the slides.
- **The Norad guide and the Itad brief are the same underlying guidance.** The
  Norad-published PDF *"A practical guide to MEL at a portfolio level"* (Sept
  2024) was **authored by Itad** (Rob Lloyd & Catrin Hepworth). The Itad article
  *"Practical guidance for portfolio-level MEL"* is the authoring firm's companion
  write-up of the same brief. They are listed separately below (as requested) but
  are not two independent documents — the structured summaries make this explicit.

## The foundational sources

### 1. Norad — *A practical guide to monitoring, evaluation and learning* (at portfolio level)

- **What it is:** A Norad Department for Evaluation **evaluation brief** (PDF,
  Sept 2024) giving practical, step-by-step guidance on putting portfolio-level
  MEL in place. Written from the **funder/agency perspective**.
- **Why it matters for funders/incubators:** It is the clearest funder-facing
  "how to" for portfolio MEL — a five-step model (theory of change → questions →
  monitoring → evaluation → learning) written *for* portfolio coordinators and
  directors, not for academics.
- 📄 [[external-sources/norad-portfolio-mel-guide-summary|Structured summary →]]
- 🔗 [Norad PDF](https://www.norad.no/contentassets/edb6d72917a84b70bf1bed3283586efc/evaluation_brief_a-practical-guide-to-monitoring-evaluation-and-learning-at-a-portfolio-level.pdf)

### 2. Itad — *Practical guidance for portfolio-level monitoring, evaluation and learning*

- **What it is:** A 2024 **learning brief / article** written by Itad **for Norad**
  (authors: Catrin Hepworth & Rob Lloyd), distilling the practical guidance from
  Itad's evaluation of Norad's portfolio management. (Same guidance as #1.)
- **Why it matters for funders/incubators:** It frames the same guidance around a
  crucial idea — use diverse **"knowledge"** (not just formal "evidence"), and
  make **analytical judgements** rather than mechanically aggregating numbers.
- 📄 [[external-sources/itad-portfolio-mel-brief-summary|Structured summary →]]
- 🔗 [Itad article](https://www.itad.com/article/practical-guidance-for-portfolio-level-monitoring-evaluation-and-learning/)

### 3. Abt Global — PMEL Nepal programme

- **What it is:** A **standing portfolio MEL function** funded by the British
  Embassy Kathmandu (FCDO Nepal) and run by Abt Global since ~2020 — a working,
  ongoing portfolio MEL facility (not a one-off evaluation).
- **Why it matters for funders/incubators:** It is the **real-world instance** of
  what the Norad/Itad guide describes — proof that a funder can embed portfolio
  MEL as a permanent capability, and a replicable operating model (it has seeded
  similar FCDO facilities in Nigeria and Kenya).
- 🔗 [Abt project page](https://www.abtglobal.com/projects/enabling-evidence-informed-donor-decision-making-in-nepal) ·
  [How PMEL Nepal strengthens a portfolio approach (blog)](https://abtgovernance.com/2025/03/24/moving-from-theory-to-practice-how-pmel-nepal-strengthens-a-portfolio-approach-to-aid-delivery-for-fcdo/) ·
  [[orgs-and-people/abt-global|Org profile →]]

### 4. Sarah Dickins (Medium) — *Learning from end to end: Integrating learning systems in portfolio monitoring and evaluation*

- **What it is:** A practitioner **Medium article** (June 2025) by Sarah Dickins (a
  mixed-methods MEL specialist) on integrating **learning systems** into portfolio
  MEL — how to connect learning end-to-end across a cluster of projects under an
  umbrella programme/fund.
- **Why it matters for funders/incubators:** It zooms in on the part funders most
  often neglect — the **learning** in MEL — and on making evidence actually get
  used across a portfolio (open question 4).
- 🔗 [Medium article](https://medium.com/@sarah.dickins_86341/learning-from-end-to-end-integrating-learning-systems-in-portfolio-monitoring-and-evaluation-50f2e045632c)
