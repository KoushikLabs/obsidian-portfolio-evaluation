> **Status:** stub — to be filled in during [[learning-plan|Week 6–7]].

## Why it belongs here

Portfolio evaluation only creates value if findings are **owned and used**.
Embedding an evaluation culture — so that evaluation is owned across an
organisation rather than bolted on — is what turns aggregate findings into
strategy decisions. This is open question 4 ("making findings actionable") seen
from the organisational side.
