# Norad (Norwegian Agency for Development Cooperation)

> The **funder/agency** voice in this knowledge base — and the commissioner of its
> foundational portfolio-MEL guide.

## Who they are

Norad is the Norwegian Agency for Development Cooperation — a government
development funder. Its **Department for Evaluation** commissions independent
evaluations of Norwegian aid and publishes guidance for the agency's own
portfolio managers.

## Why they matter for portfolio evaluation

- Norad commissioned and published *"A practical guide to monitoring, evaluation
  and learning at a portfolio level"* (Sept 2024) — the clearest, most practical,
  **funder-perspective** statement of how to do portfolio MEL in this knowledge
  base. See the [[external-sources/norad-portfolio-mel-guide-summary|structured summary]].
- The guide is written **for funders** — explicitly addressed to *"Norad portfolio
  coordinators, Section Heads and Department Directors"* — which makes it directly
  transferable to other funders and incubators.
- Norad's own honest finding: portfolio MEL was **not yet in place** in the
  organisation, and the guide proposes an integrated approach to build it. A
  useful, candid model of an agency institutionalising portfolio evaluation.

## Notable specifics

- Introduced **P-Dash**, a portfolio data-management tool, to collate partner
  evidence (a real example of dashboard/shared-measurement infrastructure).
- Defines **"knowledge" broadly** (professional, operational, technical,
  contextual) — in line with funders like Luminate and Mastercard Foundation.
