## The portfolio being evaluated

The **UK International Climate Finance (ICF) portfolio** — a large,
**multi-country** climate finance portfolio. ICF is a flagship UK funding stream
spanning many countries and interventions, making it a textbook "complex
portfolio" for evaluation.

## Methods used

Focus is on the **communication and synthesis** of a complex multi-country
evaluation rather than a specific data-collection method — i.e. how to distil and
present aggregate findings drawn from many countries and interventions.
*(Specific methods not confirmed from programme page.)*

## Key challenge addressed

The **synthesis-and-communication** problem: once you have evaluated a large,
heterogeneous, multi-country portfolio, how do you **communicate** the aggregate
findings credibly and usefully — especially to decision-makers who weren't in the
weeds? This is **open question 4** (making findings actionable) plus the
communication half of open question 2.

## Relevance to a funder / incubator

High for the **"last mile"** of portfolio evaluation. Funders consistently
struggle not with collecting evidence but with turning a complex multi-country/
multi-grant picture into something a board or strategy team can act on. The ICF
portfolio is a useful analogue for any large, geographically spread funder
portfolio.
