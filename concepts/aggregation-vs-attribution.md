# Aggregation vs attribution *(stub)*

> **Status:** stub — to be filled in during [[learning-plan|Week 3–4]].

## The distinction

- **Attribution** — claiming a specific outcome was *caused by* a specific
  intervention. Usually implausible at portfolio scale.
- **Contribution** — claiming the portfolio *plausibly contributed* to observed
  outcomes, alongside other actors and factors. The realistic standard for
  portfolios.
- **Aggregation** — combining many cases/outcomes into a portfolio-level picture
  *without* pretending to causal precision you don't have.
