## The portfolio being evaluated

**Defra's Resources & Waste Policy Programme** — a portfolio of:

- **nine policy evaluations**,
- **five thematic impact evaluations**, and
- an **overarching system-level synthesis** sitting across all of them.

A clean, concrete example of a real government policy portfolio with a deliberate
two-tier (individual studies + synthesis) structure.

## Methods used

- A **portfolio architecture** that pairs many individual evaluations with an
  **overarching system-level synthesis** — designed so the synthesis creates
  **analytical synergies** across the individual studies.
- Organisational embedding practices to make evaluation **owned across the
  organisation** rather than commissioned and shelved.
*(Specific synthesis technique not fully confirmed from the programme page.)*

## Key challenge addressed

Two linked challenges:

1. **Analytical synergy** — how portfolio-level evaluation creates value across
   individual studies (the whole being more than the sum of the parts).
2. **Embedding / ownership** — how to build an evaluation *culture* so that
   evaluation is owned across an organisation and findings actually get used
   (open question 4).

## Relevance to a funder / incubator

High for the **institutionalisation** question. Funders and incubators don't just
want a one-off portfolio evaluation — they want evaluation to become a standing
capability that informs strategy. This session is the model for *how a portfolio
evaluation can build that culture*, and the two-tier design (individual studies +
overarching synthesis) is directly reusable.
