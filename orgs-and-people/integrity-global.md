# Integrity / Integrity Global

> **The clear specialists in portfolio and mega-portfolio MEL** in this knowledge
> base. If you follow one organisation for the funder/incubator use case, follow
> Integrity.

## Who they are

Integrity (also appearing as **Integrity Global**) is a consultancy specialising
in monitoring, evaluation, research and learning (MERL) in complex,
often-conflict-affected, multi-stakeholder settings. Their distinctive strength
is **delivering MEL across large, multi-layered portfolios** — portfolios that
contain sub-portfolios, programmes and projects.

## Why they matter for portfolio evaluation

- They wrestle directly with the **core funder problem**: ill-suited criteria,
  evaluating at a high level of abstraction across heterogeneous interventions
  and actors, whether to apply OECD-DAC at portfolio scale, and defining
  evidence/success/credibility for a whole portfolio.
- They model a **"bricolage" method philosophy** — combining Outcome Harvesting,
  Contribution Analysis, Appreciative Inquiry, rubrics, quality-of-evidence
  assessment, and cross-case analysis rather than relying on one method.
