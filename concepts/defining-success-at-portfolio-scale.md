# Defining success and credibility at portfolio scale *(stub)*

> **Status:** stub — to be filled in during [[learning-plan|Week 4]].

## The question

What counts as **success** for a *portfolio* (as opposed to a project)? And what
counts as a **credible** evidence base and a credible *claim* when reasoning at a
high level of abstraction across many interventions and actors?

## To fill in

- [ ] Portfolio success ≠ sum of project successes. Possible portfolio-level
  success definitions: strategic coverage, learning generated, systemic
  influence, diversified risk, a few outsized wins, coherence/synergy.
- [ ] Credibility of evidence: quality-of-evidence assessment, triangulation,
  transparency about confidence.
- [ ] Credibility of claims: contribution stories that name assumptions and rival
  explanations.
- [ ] For incubators: cohort-level success (survival, follow-on funding, learning
  velocity) vs single-venture wins.
