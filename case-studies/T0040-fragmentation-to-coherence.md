## The portfolio being evaluated

Not a single named portfolio — the session is about **MEL assessments** that have
become *fragmented* (many disconnected assessments) and how to move them toward
*coherence*. The "portfolio" here is effectively the funder's or programme's whole
body of MEL activity.

## Methods used

Reframing/redesign of how MEL assessments are structured so they cohere rather
than fragment. *(Specific methods not confirmed from programme page.)*

## Key challenge addressed

**Fragmentation** — the failure mode where a portfolio's MEL is a pile of
disconnected assessments with no coherent whole. Directly relevant to the
"disconnected reporting" failure mode described in
[[concepts/the-heterogeneity-problem|the heterogeneity problem]].

## Relevance to a funder / incubator

Moderate–high. Funders routinely end up with fragmented grant-by-grant reporting
and no portfolio-level coherence. This session names that problem and the move
toward coherence — a precondition for any useful portfolio synthesis.
