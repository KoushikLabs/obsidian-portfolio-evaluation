# Appreciative Inquiry

## What it is

**Appreciative Inquiry (AI)** is a strengths-based, generative approach to inquiry
and change. Rather than starting from problems and deficits, it starts from
*what is working* and *what gives life to a system at its best*, and uses that as
the basis for learning and improvement. Its classic cycle is the **4-D model**:
**Discover** (what works), **Dream** (what could be), **Design** (what should be),
**Destiny/Deliver** (what will be).

## Why it matters for portfolio evaluation

- In a portfolio of heterogeneous grants, an appreciative lens is a practical way
  to **surface positive deviance and transferable lessons** across very different
  interventions — *"what is working, for whom, and why?"* — which is hard to do
  with a purely compliance-driven frame.
- It changes the **politics of evaluation**: grantees and ventures engage more
  openly when the inquiry is framed generatively, which improves data quality and
  the chance that findings get used (open question 4).
- It supports **evaluation culture** ([[case-studies/T0098-building-evaluation-culture|Defra case]])
  by making evaluation feel developmental rather than judgemental.

## When to use it

- As one strand in a **"bricolage"** portfolio evaluation, alongside Outcome
  Harvesting and Contribution Analysis (see
  [[case-studies/T0029-brush-strokes-to-bold-strokes|T0029]], which combines
  exactly these three).
- Incubator cohorts: drawing out what the strongest ventures and the strongest
  cohort experiences had in common.
- Early/formative portfolio learning, where the goal is improvement not judgement.

## Limitations / cautions

- Risk of **positivity bias** — must be deliberately balanced with what isn't
  working and with honest, summative judgement. The Norad/Itad guide's learning
  frameworks pair "what worked well? why?" with "what worked less well? why?"
  precisely to avoid this.
- Not a substitute for evidence quality assessment or contribution logic; it is a
  *posture and process*, not a causal method.
