# One-Page "Evaluation for Non-Evaluator Funders" Brief — Template

> A **one-page** brief to communicate portfolio-evaluation findings to people who
> are *not* evaluators — funders, boards, investment committees, incubator
> leadership. Optimised for a decision-maker who has five minutes and one
> question: *"so what do we do?"*
>
> Inspired by the synthesis-and-communication challenge in
> [[case-studies/T0102-icf-portfolio-communication|T0102 (ICF portfolio)]]
> and the "learning gets used" emphasis of
> [[external-sources/external-sources-index|Sarah Dickins]]. **Keep it to one page.**

---

## «Portfolio name» — Portfolio Evaluation: What We Found & What To Do

**For:** «audience» **·** **Date:** «…» **·** **Prepared by:** «…»

### The bottom line *(3 sentences max)*
> «Across the portfolio, the headline judgement is … . The strongest evidence is
> for … . The main decision this points to is … .»

### What the portfolio is achieving *(plain language, no jargon)*
- **Working well:** «2–4 bullets — what's contributing, with confidence level»
- **Working less well / uncertain:** «2–4 bullets — honest about gaps & ambiguity»
- **Our contribution:** «what the funder plausibly contributed — contribution, not
  attribution. State confidence.»

### The evidence behind this *(one short paragraph)*
> «How we know — what was synthesised, how many cases, how strong the evidence is
> overall, and the biggest caveat. One sentence on method, in lay terms.»

### What this means for decisions *(the "so what")*

| Decision | Recommendation | Why (evidence) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| «Scale?» | «…» | «…» | H/M/L |
| «Stop / redirect?» | «…» | «…» | H/M/L |
| «Strategy / next cohort?» | «…» | «…» | H/M/L |

### Where we're flying blind *(evidence gaps worth funding)*
- «1–3 gaps — what we *can't* yet say, and what it would take to find out»

---

> **Optional visual:** a small portfolio map / Evidence Gap Map / RAG-rated
> outcome table can replace several bullets — one good visual beats a paragraph for
> this audience.

### Drafting rules (delete before sending)
- One page. If it's longer, you're writing for evaluators, not funders.
- Lead with the decision, not the methodology.
- No undefined jargon (no "contribution analysis", "OECD-DAC", "rubric" unless
  defined in half a sentence).
- Always show **confidence** — non-evaluators trust honest uncertainty more than
  false precision.
- Every recommendation must trace to evidence in the
  [[templates/cross-case-analysis-matrix|cross-case matrix]].
