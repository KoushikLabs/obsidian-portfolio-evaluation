# Cross-Case Analysis Matrix — Template

> A cross-case analysis matrix is how you turn a pile of individual grants/
> ventures into **portfolio-level patterns**. Each row is a case; the columns are
> the *same* dimensions for every case, so you can read *down* a column to see
> patterns and *across* a row to understand one case. See
> [[methods/contribution-analysis|methods/contribution-analysis]] and
> [[methods/outcome-harvesting|methods/outcome-harvesting]].

## How to use it

1. Define the **columns** from your portfolio theory of change and evaluation
   questions (keep them identical across all cases — that's what makes it
   *cross*-case).
2. Fill one **row per grant/venture/case**.
3. Rate the evaluative columns against your [[methods/rubrics|rubric]], and
   record the **quality/confidence of evidence** behind each rating.
4. Read **down** each column to surface patterns; cluster cases; identify positive
   deviance, common enablers/barriers, and gaps.
5. Feed the patterns into the [[templates/funder-brief-template|funder brief]].

## Pattern-reading worksheet (fill after the matrix)

- **Patterns by cluster/type:** «which types of intervention performed how?»
- **Patterns by context:** «what worked *for whom, in which contexts*?»
- **Common enablers across high-contribution cases:** «…»
- **Common barriers across low-contribution cases:** «…»
- **Positive deviance (surprising successes) & what explains them:** «…»
- **Negative/null outcomes (deliberately surfaced):** «…»
- **Evidence gaps (cells where confidence is Low):** «… → candidate for an
  [[methods/evidence-gap-maps|Evidence Gap Map]]»
- **Portfolio-level contribution story (drawing the cases together):** «…»

---

## Credibility checklist

- [ ] Same columns applied to every case (no cherry-picked dimensions).
- [ ] Ratings tied to the [[methods/rubrics|rubric]], not gut feel.
- [ ] Quality-of-evidence recorded per cell; low-confidence cells flagged, not hidden.
- [ ] Negative/ambiguous cases included.
- [ ] Patterns triangulated across more than one case before being claimed.
