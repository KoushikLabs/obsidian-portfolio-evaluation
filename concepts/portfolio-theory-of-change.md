## Why it matters

You cannot evaluate a portfolio at the aggregate level without an articulated
**portfolio-level theory of change (ToC)** — the strategic logic explaining how a
diverse set of interventions is *collectively* expected to contribute to
higher-level outcomes. It is the backbone that lets you set portfolio-level
evaluation questions and decide what to synthesise.
