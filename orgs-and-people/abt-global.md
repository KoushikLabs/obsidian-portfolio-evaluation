# Abt Global

> The best live example in this knowledge base of a **standing portfolio MEL
> function** — portfolio evaluation as an ongoing capability, not a one-off study.

## Who they are

Abt Global (formerly Abt Associates; UK entity Abt Britain Ltd) is an
international development and research firm. In the portfolio-evaluation context
they are notable for running **Portfolio Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning
(PMEL)** facilities for funders.

## Why they matter for portfolio evaluation

- They operate the **Nepal PMEL programme**, a standing portfolio MEL function
  funded by the **British Embassy Kathmandu (BEK) / FCDO Nepal** and implemented
  by Abt Global. Running since ~2020 (in its fifth year as of 2025), it is a rare,
  well-documented example of an **embedded, ongoing** portfolio MEL function.
- PMEL Nepal does exactly the three things a funder needs at portfolio level:
  1. **Align programme-level MEL** to maximise the *aggregate and additive* value
     of MEL data programmes already collect (rather than re-measuring).
  2. **Synthesise evidence** and create new knowledge products that answer
     *portfolio-level* questions.
  3. **Facilitate learning systems and processes** for shared learning and better
     portfolio decisions — the "big picture" view for leadership.
- The model has been influential enough to **inspire other FCDO PMEL facilities**
  (e.g. Nigeria, Kenya) — evidence that a standing portfolio MEL function is
  replicable.

## Why this is the key contrast with the Norad/Itad guide

The [[external-sources/norad-portfolio-mel-guide-summary|Norad/Itad guidance]]
describes what portfolio MEL *should* look like (and notes Norad hadn't yet built
it). **Abt's PMEL Nepal is a working instance of it** — a funder actually running
a standing portfolio MEL function. Read them together: Norad = the blueprint,
PMEL Nepal = the building.
