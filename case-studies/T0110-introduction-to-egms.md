## The portfolio being evaluated

A methods session rather than a portfolio case — but EGMs are applied *to*
portfolios/evidence bases, mapping interventions against outcomes to reveal
coverage and gaps.

## Methods used

[[methods/evidence-gap-maps|**Evidence Gap Maps**]] — structured matrices of
interventions × outcomes showing where evidence is abundant, thin, or absent.

## Key challenge addressed

How to make a heterogeneous evidence base / portfolio **legible at a glance** and
spot where evidence (or coverage) is missing — open questions 1 and 2 (criteria/
coverage and synthesis).

## Relevance to a funder / incubator

High and very practical. A funder can build an EGM over its own portfolio to see
what it covers, where it is over-concentrated, and where it is making
under-evidenced bets — a direct input to strategy and grant selection.
