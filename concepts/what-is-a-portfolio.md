# What is a portfolio? *(stub)*

> **Status:** stub — to be filled in during [[learning-plan|Week 1]].

## Working definition

A *portfolio* is a set of interventions (grants, projects, ventures) that share a
**common strategic intent** but differ in their activities, contexts, and
definitions of success. The portfolio — not any single intervention — is the
unit of analysis.
