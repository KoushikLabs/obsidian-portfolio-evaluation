# Ipsos

## Who they are

Ipsos is a global market- and social-research firm with a substantial **policy
and programme evaluation** practice. In the portfolio-evaluation context they
appear as evaluators of large **government policy portfolios** and as advocates
for **building evaluation culture** inside commissioning organisations.

## Why they matter for portfolio evaluation

- They demonstrate portfolio evaluation in a **public-policy** setting (a close
  analogue to a large institutional funder).
- Their emphasis on **embedding evaluation across an organisation** — so it is
  owned, not just commissioned — speaks directly to the funder/incubator question
  of how to *institutionalise* portfolio evaluation (open question 4).
