# Itad

> Authored the **foundational portfolio-MEL guidance** in this knowledge base —
> the Norad practical guide. A primary org to follow.

## Who they are

Itad is a specialist international-development MEL consultancy, well known for
**theory-based evaluation**, **contribution analysis**, and complex/systems-aware
evaluation. They frequently work at the **portfolio and strategy level** for
funders and development agencies.

## Why they matter for portfolio evaluation

- They **wrote the book** (well, the brief) on portfolio MEL for a major funder:
  Itad's **Rob Lloyd** and **Catrin Hepworth** authored the Norad evaluation brief
  *"A practical guide to monitoring, evaluation and learning at a portfolio
  level"* (Sept 2024) — see the structured summary in
  [[external-sources/norad-portfolio-mel-guide-summary|external-sources]].
- That brief grew out of Itad's (with **Chr. Michelsen Institute** and **SEI**)
  evaluation *"Evaluation of Norad's use of knowledge in portfolio management"*
  (Norad Report 4/2024).
- Itad are leading practitioners of **contribution analysis** — the default causal
  logic for portfolio evaluation.

## Named people

- **Catrin Hepworth** — co-author of the brief.
