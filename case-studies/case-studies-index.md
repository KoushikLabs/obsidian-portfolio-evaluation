# Case Studies

Structured write-ups of real portfolio-evaluation work. The core set comes from
the **UK Evaluation Society 2026 conference** programme, which had a genuine
cluster of sessions on portfolio-level and cross-programme evaluation — directly
relevant to the funder/incubator use case.

Each file uses a common structure:

- **Title**
- **Presenter(s)**
- **Organisation**
- **The portfolio being evaluated**
- **Methods used**
- **Key challenge addressed**
- **Relevance to a funder / incubator**

> ⚠️ **Source note.** These write-ups are based on the public conference
> programme page (evaluation.org.uk) and the session abstracts. The individual
> abstract detail pages on nomadit.co.uk were outside approved domains and were
> not accessed. The **conference slides** themselves (in the
> `Conference-2026-slides-for-sharing/` folder) are a **private,
> personal-reference-only source — see the [[external-sources/external-sources-index|external-sources note]]
> and must not be redistributed.** Where a field could not be confirmed from the
> programme page, it is marked *(not confirmed from programme)*.

## The recurring tension

Across **every** session, the same tension appears — and it is the same one a
funder or incubator will hit:

> **How do you say something credible and useful at the *aggregate* level when the
> underlying grants/ventures are *heterogeneous*?**
