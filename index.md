# Portfolio Evaluation — Wiki Index

*Last updated: 2026-06-19 · Concepts: 6 · Methods: 6 · Case studies: 10 · Orgs & people: 6 · External sources: 2 · Templates: 4*

A knowledge base for evaluating **portfolios** of diverse interventions — for
**funders and incubators** assessing grant portfolios and accelerator cohorts at
an *aggregate* level.

## The four open questions (the spine)

1. **Setting criteria** across heterogeneous interventions. *(Q1)*
2. **Synthesising** across cases. *(Q2)*
3. **Defining success & credibility** at portfolio scale. *(Q3)*
4. **Making findings actionable** for funders. *(Q4)*
