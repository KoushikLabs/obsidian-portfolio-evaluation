# The heterogeneity problem *(stub)*

> **Status:** stub — the central tension of this whole knowledge base.

## The problem in one sentence

How do you say something **credible and useful at the aggregate level** when the
underlying grants or ventures are genuinely **heterogeneous** — different
sectors, stages, geographies, theories of change, and timescales?

## The two failure modes to avoid

1. **Meaningless averaging** — forcing incomparable interventions onto one scale
   and reporting a portfolio "score" that hides more than it reveals.
2. **Disconnected reporting** — a pile of individual project reports with no
   portfolio-level story or synthesis.
