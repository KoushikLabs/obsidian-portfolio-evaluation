# Greo Evidence Insights

## Who they are

Greo Evidence Insights (commonly **Greo**) is an evidence and evaluation
organisation focused on **knowledge mobilisation** and **embedding evaluation**
within networks and systems of organisations. Their work often centres on shared
measurement and on making evidence usable across many actors.

## Why they matter for portfolio evaluation

- They specialise in the **network/collective** version of the portfolio problem:
  how to evaluate at the collective level across many independent actors.
- Their use of **collective-impact pillars** and **dashboards** is a concrete,
  reusable model for **shared measurement** — directly relevant to incubator
  cohorts and collaborative funders.
- They are strong on the **embedding/ownership** side (open question 4) — making
  evaluation a shared, living practice rather than an external audit.
