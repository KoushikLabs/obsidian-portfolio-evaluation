## The portfolio being evaluated

A methods/innovation session — about the *technique* of Outcome Harvesting
augmented with AI, applicable to any portfolio where you harvest outcomes from
many informants.

## Methods used

- [[methods/outcome-harvesting|**Outcome Harvesting**]] — the core method.
- **AI interviewers** — using AI to conduct or assist the informant interviews
  that feed the harvest (potentially scaling outcome collection across a large,
  heterogeneous portfolio).

## Key challenge addressed

The **cost and scale** of harvesting outcomes across many grantees/ventures —
informant interviews are the bottleneck. AI interviewing is offered as a way to
scale the data-collection step of a portfolio outcome harvest.

## Relevance to a funder / incubator

High and forward-looking. Outcome Harvesting is ideal for heterogeneous
portfolios but labour-intensive; AI-assisted interviewing could make it feasible
at portfolio scale. Directly connects to using Claude in the workflow.
